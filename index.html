Two timelines, one recommended route

The chronological stage is keyed to completion prerequisites, not simply the earliest area you can visit. Finish child-only tasks before the first time jump when practical. Visit the other age again after Forest Temple.

Switch 2 remake differences

Five bottles, Echo Gem at 20 Gold Skulltulas, moved Great Fairy rewards, new Fairy Note heart puzzles, Hall of Fallen Foes, and revised layouts. The linked videos are of the 2026 remake.
